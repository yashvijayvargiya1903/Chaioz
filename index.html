01 / The Chaioz world

A recipe that travelled.
A place that stayed.

Chaioz brings an Indian chai-café ritual to North Adelaide — the kind of place where the first sip slows you down and one more snack somehow becomes the plan.

The brand describes its chai as a family recipe carried from India to O'Connell Street, with hand-blended spices, slow brewing and a late-night spirit.
