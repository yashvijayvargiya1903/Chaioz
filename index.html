02 · OUR STORYFROM INDIA → ADELAIDE

Not just chai.
A place to belong.

Every cup at Chaioz starts with a recipe that travelled from a kitchen in India to Adelaide's O'Connell Street — same spices, same ratio, same stubborn love for doing it slowly.

Chaioz brings Indian chai culture together with modern café energy: karak, street food, milkcakes, late-night specials and a space that keeps the door open for connection.
